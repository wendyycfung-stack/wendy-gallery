序 — 從醫護到業餘畫家

畢業於香港養和護士學校，曾在香港養和、港中及仁安私家醫院工作多年，期後任職於澳門科技大學醫院擔任總護士長一職，及後到中國河北石家庄婦幼醫院擔任醫院副院長及總護士長。終於結束醫院生涯。

於2008年至2011年間為香港兩間安老院同時擔任兩院副院長；2012至2015年在香港擔任香港乳癌基金會服務總監；其後曾分別任職中、西醫療集團營運總監一職；2019年開始為全球華人乳癌組織聯盟之服務總監，亦同時兼任香港品質保證局評審員及恩耆頤養院顧問。

多年來從護士到醫療管理，營營役役，奔走於醫療工作。慢慢開始為自己制定每年目標，或計劃每年參與兩個興趣活動，去1-2個國家旅行。年尾檢討目標是否已達成，年頭再設新目標。如是已成習慣並推行多年。直到現在人生已達收成期，也可以為自己而活得更精采，到目前為止，興趣還不少：打golf，上古箏，中樂，另外就是畫畫了。

朋友經常會讚我有藝術天份，其實我更相信是自己多年來未停過向前邁進，努力與堅持令我由一個小小護士做到總護士長及醫院副院長，也見證自己無論在古箏、中樂及畫畫方面都有進步。最開心的是老師的讚賞，功夫沒有白費，但真正可以留下作記念的只有畫作。

這幾年在構思自製一本回憶錄，將多年經驗及心得留下。隨着時代轉變，少有人閱讀書籍，反而多溜覽網站，時至今日科技發展千里，讓我盟生製作自己網頁，儲存工作心得，並插入近期畫畫作品，讓溜覽我網站的朋友不致枯燥乏味。既有此想法，在詢問很多朋友仍未學到如何製作網站，終於親自報讀了一個「自製網站不求人」，開始慢慢學習與AI溝通，將心血放入這網站，感覺一生不枉過。

也希望我的後人像我一樣永不放棄，活得精彩。

Foreword — From Healthcare Professional to Amateur Painter

Graduated from the Hong Kong Sanatorium & Hospital School of Nursing, and worked for many years at HKS&H, HKCH and Union Hospital. Subsequently served as Chief Nursing Officer at the University Hospital of Macau University of Science and Technology, and later as Assistant Superintendent and Chief Nursing Officer at Shijiazhuang Maternity and Child Health Hospital in Hebei, China. Finally ended my hospital career.

From 2008 to 2011, simultaneously served as Assistant Superintendent of two elderly homes in Hong Kong. From 2012 to 2015, served as Service Director of the HKBCF. Afterwards, held the position of Operations Director at both Chinese and Western medical groups respectively. Starting from 2019, served as Service Director of the GCBC. Also concurrently serves as an assessor for the Hong Kong Quality Assurance Agency and a consultant for a Nursing Home.

Over the years, from nurse to healthcare management, toiling away, constantly on the go in medical work. Gradually began setting annual goals for myself, or planning to participate in two interest activities each year and travel to 1-2 countries. At year-end, reviewing whether goals have been achieved, and at the start of the year, setting new goals. This become a habit and has been carried out for many years.

Until now, life has reached its harvest period, and I can also live more wonderfully for myself. To date, I still have quite a few interests: playing golf, learning the guzheng, Chinese music, and additionally, painting.

Friends often praise me for having artistic talent, but I actually believe more that it is my years of never stopping moving forward—effort and persistence—that have taken me from a RN to CNO and hospital Assistant Superintendent, and also witnessed my progress in guzheng, Chinese music, and painting. What makes me happiest is my teacher's praise—my efforts have not been in vain, but what can truly remain as a memento are only my paintings.

In recent years, I have been conceptualizing a self-made memoir to preserve years of experience and insights. As times change, few people read books anymore; instead, more browse websites. Today's technology has developed by leaps and bounds, which has inspired me to create my own website, storing work insights and inserting recent painting works, so that friends who browse my website won't find it dull and boring. With this idea in mind, after asking many friends who have no idea in building a website, I finally enrolled in a course called "Build Your Own Website Without Help" and began slowly learning to communicate with AI, putting my heart and soul into this website, feeling that my life has not been lived in vain.

I also hope that my descendants, like me, will never give up and live life to the fullest.
