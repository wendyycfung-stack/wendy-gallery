序 — 從醫護到業餘畫家

畢業於香港養和護士學校，曾在香港養和、港中及仁安私家醫院工作多年，期後任職於澳門科技大學醫院擔任總護士長一職，及後到中國河北石家庄婦幼醫院擔任醫院副院長及總護士長。終於結束醫院生涯。 於2008 年至2011年間為香港兩間安老院同時擔任兩院副院長。 2012至2015年在香港擔任香港乳癌基金會服務總監。 其後曾分別任職中、西醫療集團營運總監一職。 2019年開始為全球華人乳癌組織聯盟之服務總監。亦同時兼任香港品質保證局評審員及恩耆頤養院顧問。 多年來從護士到醫療管理，營營役役，奔走於醫療工作。慢慢開始為自己制定每年目標，或計劃每年參與兩個興趣活動，去1-2個國家旅行。 年尾檢討目標是否已達成，年頭再設新目標。如是已推行多年計劃。 直到現在人生已達收成期，也可以為自己而活得更精采，到目前為止，興趣還不少：打golf，上古箏，中樂，另外就是畫畫了。 朋友經常會讚我有藝術天份，其實我更相信是自己多年來未停過向前邁進，努力與堅持令我由一個小小護士做到總護士長及醫院副院長，也見證自己無論在古箏、中樂及畫畫方面都有進步。最開心的是老師的讚賞，功夫沒有白費，但真正可以留下作記念的只有畫作，這幾年在構思自製一本畫册記錄畫畫歷程，但今日科技發展千里，讓我盟生製作自己網頁儲存作品，既有此想法，在詢問很多朋友仍未學到如何製作網站，終於親自上了一個「自製網站不求人」，開始慢慢將心血放入這網站，感覺一生不枉過。 也希望我的後人像我一樣永不放棄，活得精彩。 Foreword — From Healthcare Professional to Amateur Painter A graduate of the Hong Kong Sanatorium & Hospital School of Nursing, I spent many years working at private hospitals in Hong Kong—including Hong Kong Sanatorium & Hospital, Hong Kong Central Hospital, and Union Hospital. Subsequently, I served as Nursing Director at the Macau University of Science and Technology Hospital, followed by a role as Deputy Hospital Director and Chief Nursing Officer at the Shijiazhuang Maternal and Child Health Hospital in Hebei, China. Eventually, I concluded my career in hospital administration. From 2008 to 2011, I served concurrently as the Deputy Director of two elderly care homes in Hong Kong. From 2012 to 2015, I held the position of Service Director at the Hong Kong Breast Cancer Foundation. Subsequently, I served as the Operations Director for both Traditional Chinese Medicine and Western medicine healthcare groups. Since 2019, I had held the position of Service Director for the Global Chinese Breast Cancer Organizations Alliance. Concurrently, I also serve as an assessor for the Hong Kong Quality Assurance Agency and as a consultant for Yan Kei Elderly Care Home. Having spent years dedicated to a demanding career—transitioning from nursing to healthcare management—I gradually began setting annual personal goals, such as participating in two hobby-related activities and traveling to one or two countries each year. I review the achievement of these goals at the end of the year and sets new ones at the start of the next—this seems like a habit that I have maintained for many years. Now that I have reached the harvest years of my life, I can live more vibrantly for myself. I have a wide range of interests: playing golf, learning the guzheng, engaging with traditional Chinese music, and painting. Friends often compliment me on my artistic talent; however, I attribute my achievements more to my relentless drive to move forward. Hard work and perseverance propelled me from a staff nurse to a Chief Nursing Officer and Deputy Hospital Director, and I have also witnessed my own progress in the guzheng, traditional Chinese music, and painting. While I cherish the praise from my teachers—proof that my efforts were not in vain—it is my paintings that serve as lasting mementos. For years, I had envisioned creating an album to document my artistic journey. With the rapid advancement of technology, the idea of ​​building my own website to showcase my work took root. After consulting many friends without successfully learning how to build a site, I finally took a "DIY Website Creation" course myself. As I gradually pour my heart and soul into this website, I feel that my life has been truly fulfilling. I also hope that my descendants will, like me, never give up and will live their lives to the fullest.
